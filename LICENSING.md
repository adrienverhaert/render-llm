being verified. Public availability of source is not itself a license grant.

authorship.

Image Denoise, oneTBB and DirectX Shader Compiler. Names and runtime hashes alone
are not a redistribution inventory. Exact versions, notices, source/relink
requirements and any bundled asset terms must be verified for the binary being
distributed. Do not redistribute the old executable or label a package MIT-only.
